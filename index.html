THE EIGHTEEN HOLY STEPS

✦

The Pathinettam Padi lead to Lord Ayyappa’s sanctum at Sabarimala. In Padi Pooja, each step is worshipped as a stage the devotee rises above on the way to the Lord.
Tap a step to light it.

Step 1

Tap any step to read its meaning.

1–5 · Five senses 6–13 · Eight passions 14–16 · Three gunas 17–18 · Knowledge & ignorance

One traditional interpretation. Others link the steps to Ayyappa’s eighteen weapons or the eighteen hills around Sabarimala.
